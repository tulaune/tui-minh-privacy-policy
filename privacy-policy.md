# Privacy Policy — Tụi Mình

**Effective date:** 22 October 2026

Tụi Mình (also displayed as **Our Life Passport**) is a local-first mobile
application published by the developer of this app. This Privacy Policy
explains what information the current release handles and how it is protected.

## Information stored on your device

The current release can store information that you choose to create or import,
such as:

- memories, notes, dates, tags, and privacy labels;
- photos that you explicitly choose from your device; and
- app preferences and local settings.

This information is stored locally on your device for the app's core features.
The current release does not require an account and does not upload this
content to a Tụi Mình server.

## Information collected or shared

The current release does not collect or share personal information with the
developer or third parties for advertising, analytics, profiling, or sale. It
does not contain advertising. AI features are unavailable in this release, so
the app does not send your content to an AI provider from the production
release.

Google Play may process information as part of its own store, installation,
security, and distribution services. Those activities are governed by Google's
own policies.

## Permissions and user choices

The app only accesses content that you explicitly choose through the relevant
Android system picker or app interaction. You can refuse optional access, and
you can remove imported content from the app or from your device at any time.

## Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it in the app where that
option is available, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Because the
current release does not create an online account or upload this content to a
Tụi Mình server, there is no developer-hosted account data to delete.

## Security

The app relies on Android's application storage protections and your device's
security controls for local data. No method of storage is completely secure,
so keep your device, operating system, and screen lock protected.

## Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before any future release changes how information
is accessed, collected, used, or shared. The effective date above identifies
the latest version.
